**Deadlines inside two weeks:** Défi Aérospatial Étudiant applications close
31 October, Make AI Happen (KPMG) closes 6 November, Terror Jam closes 23 October.

## Events

**H Company × 42AI — Computer Use Agents Hackathon** — 36 hours building an agent
that drives a real desktop or application; Paris, 7–8 Nov 2026. Free, teams of
1–4, entry by application. **€10,000 in cash** — the largest student prize in France I
could find, and pure engineering rather than a pitch.
[lu.ma/hz9db5u9](https://lu.ma/hz9db5u9)

**Make AI Happen (KPMG France)** — student challenge on three briefs, one of them a team of collaborating AI
agents; remote until the finale on 7 Dec at KPMG Paris La Défense. Applications close 6 Nov, teams of 2–4, free.
Prizes are **gift cards, not cash**: €2,000, €1,000 and €500, plus a fast-track
recruiting interview.
[agorize.com](https://www.agorize.com/en/challenges/make-ai-happen)

**Défi Aérospatial Étudiant, 21st edition** — a year-long team project on a
reusable suborbital vehicle or an orbital vehicle servicing LEO, with ten work
packages (propulsion, mission profile, medicine, law, supply chain), closing with
an in-person Challenge Day on 3 June 2027. Applications run 7–31 Oct 2026;
prizes are the ESA Grand Prix plus ArianeGroup, CNES and Dassault Aviation awards. [studentaerospacechallenge.eu](https://www.studentaerospacechallenge.eu/)

**Hackathon AI for Health** — three mandatory in-person days turning medical
challenges into AI prototypes; Métafore Boétie, Paris, 13–15 Nov 2026. The
winner pitches on the Adopt AI stage at the Grand Palais on 4 Dec. Five awards,
cash amounts unstated; solo or teams.
[agorize.com](https://hackathons-artefactadoptai.agorize.com/en/challenges/hackathon-adopt-ai-for-health)

**Drone Defense Hackathon** — Europe's biggest drone hackathon: 48 hours in a
1,000 m² drone cage at the Grand Palais, Paris, 24–26 Nov 2026, four challenges
(autonomy mid-conflict, multi-drone coordination, detection, emergency
delivery). Teams of 6–8; the team call closed 15 Oct, the online phase runs to
23 Nov. No cash prize published.
[agorize.com](https://hackathons-artefactadoptai.agorize.com/en/challenges/drone-defense-hackathon)

**Humanoid Defense Robot Hackathon** — the Defence Innovation Agency (ministry of
the Armed Forces) with Artefact; Grand Palais, Paris, 2–4 Dec 2026. **€6,000
total, €3,000 per track**, teams of 6–8, on SLAM and GPS-denied navigation or
precision manipulation and logistics, simulated then tested on real humanoids with
Naval Group, Wandercraft and the GIGN. This round's teams are selected; the next
call opens each autumn.
[agorize.com](https://hackathons-artefactadoptai.agorize.com/en/challenges/hackathon-robotique-defense1)

**Hack1Robo 5** — 48 hours mixing AI, robotics, cognitive science and art, run
by the neuro campus of Université de Bordeaux with Inria; Station Marne,
Bordeaux, 20–22 Nov 2026. Free, teams of 4–6, with Thymio robots and motion capture provided. No cash prize published.
[hack1robo.fr](https://www.hack1robo.fr/)

**HABS Neuro-AI Hackathon** — four weeks on real EEG datasets with HABS and
Université Paris-Saclay; Orsay, 2–30 Nov 2026, awards on 14 Dec. For M1/M2 and
engineering students from their second year onwards; the form is open but the
rules are still being finalised. Top three win prizes plus internship and
recruitment tracks. [hackathon.habs.ai](https://hackathon.habs.ai/)

Also seen: [Sciences Po AI Hackathon](https://www.sciencespo.fr/ecole-management-impact/fr/evenements/the-new-sciences-po-ai-hackathon-edition/) (registration opens 3 Nov) ·
[Hackathon Pépite](https://www.sorbonne-universite.fr/evenements/hackathon-pepite-villes-durables-et-responsables) (€1,200/900/750) ·

Sweep note: Paris dominates; the Adopt AI ecosystem at the Grand Palais is the
richest vein — five student hackathons with real cash. Outside Paris the set is
thin, because most French student AI and robotics competitions (OFIA, Prologin,
CDR Junior) are legally lycée-only, and announcements live on Instagram and
Discord.

## Game jams

Six, ranked by actual money:

- **Terror Jam 2026** — 10–23 Oct, online (browser or Windows), solo or teams of
  1–5. Cash: $500 best overall, $200 best core gameplay, $200 scariest, $100
  people's choice, $100 best design trial. **AI disqualifies you from the prize
  pool**, so this one is hand-made assets only.
  [itch.io](https://itch.io/jam/terror-jam-2026-a-halloween-game-jam)
- **Game Gauntlet – SIM Jam** — 23 Sep–4 Nov, fully online, teams up to 5, joining
  the Discord is mandatory. $1,500 cash ($1,000 first, $500 second), plus a Unity
  mentorship prize and a $500 Farclip prize pool.
  [itch.io](https://itch.io/jam/game-gauntlet-sim-jam)
- **Slapjam #2** — 29–31 Oct (UTC), online, 48 hours, mobile portrait HTML5. $1,000
  cash pool (1st $500, 2nd $200, 4th $100) plus tool subscriptions. AI is
  required and credited, not banned. [itch.io](https://itch.io/jam/slapjam-ai-2)
- **Micro Jam 068: Evolution** — 30 Oct–2 Nov, online, 48 hours, solo or teams of
  up to 4. $300 cash pool, and AI is explicitly allowed.
  [itch.io](https://itch.io/jam/micro-jam-068)
- **GDevelop BIG Game Jam #10** — 6–15 Nov, online, 9 days, must use GDevelop.
  Cash for the top teams (amounts unpublished; last edition paid €400 and €250),
  engine subscriptions, and 1,000 in-engine credits for every submission.
  [itch.io](https://itch.io/jam/gdevelop-big-game-jam-10)
- **Pixel Forge MEGA JAM** — 28 Nov–8 Dec, online, 10 days, teams of up to 4. Only the top three are money ($300/$200/$100); everything below is software
  licences, cloud credits and vouchers, and AI is banned.
  [itch.io](https://itch.io/jam/pixel-forge-mega-jam)

Context: a September survey of 27 jams found eight banning or limiting generative
AI, seven allowing it and nine requiring it; rules move between editions, so read
the page for the edition you are entering.
[slapjam.ai](https://slapjam.ai/news/is-ai-allowed-in-game-jams/)

## AI

- **Meta opened Muse Spark Open** (15 Oct): 816B total / 42B active MoE, 1M
  context, weights under Meta's own Muse Model License (attribution required, not
  OSI). Meta calls it the most capable US open-weight model; AA measures HLE 43.88
  and GPQA 88.99 at xhigh effort.
- **MiniMax M3.1 Flash** (19 Oct): $0.15/$0.60 per million tokens, 1M context,
  weights promised for 23 Oct, so API-only today. AA measures 48% on HLE at max
  effort and 42 on its Intelligence Index, 13 points above M3, at roughly $0.22
  per index task against M3's ~$0.51. Its DeepSWE 73.7 is vendor-reported, not rerun.
- **Inception Mercury 3** (19 Oct): a diffusion LM claiming 1,068 tok/s — OpenRouter
  measures about 223 tok/s at p50 — and beating GPT-6 Luna and Claude Haiku 5.5
  on 50 Mind2Web tasks. No benchmark scores published; its default effort is
  "low".
- **Reflection AI's Beam 1.0** (weights on 16 Oct, Apache 2.0): 501B total / 23B
  active, text-only. Vendor-reported GPQA 90.5 and SWE-bench 80.9, no independent rerun.
- **Mistral Large 4** (1.05T, ~490B active) opens its weights on 27 Oct; its launch
  table was self-reported only (DeepSWE v1.1 61.7).

One benchmark, one source, but not one setting: effort is "max" unless noted,
Muse Spark Open at xhigh, Haiku 5.5 without tools.

## Geopolitics

- **Japan funds two GlobalFoundries fabs in Malta, New York** — $10bn for the first
  (online 2029), up to $21bn for both — while Commerce takes a ~4% stake: the state
  is now a shareholder in the chip foundry.
- **Hormuz:** the IRGC says two tankers carrying ~2m barrels each hit naval mines and
  caught fire (independently unconfirmed); Brent sits near $108, a five-month high.
  Chokepoint risk is priced, not hypothetical.
- **Treasury's first Outbound Investment penalty** (7 Oct): $200,000 against Amidi
  for not reporting a ~$92,478 investment by its Chinese fund into a Chinese
  embodied-AI startup — twice the investment, no de minimis threshold.
- **China's Q3 GDP grew 4.5%** and September industrial output rose 5.5%, which
  undercuts the story that export controls are forcing a slowdown.
- **Trump's diesel deal with Russia** delays sanctions to protect Ukraine talks,
  and a congressional bill to block it is moving: sanctions traded for a
  negotiation channel.
