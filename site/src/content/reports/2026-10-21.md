## AI

- **DeepSeek open-sourced DeepInfer** (today, Apache 2.0): the production
  inference framework behind V4.1-Flash — FlashMLA attention, DeepGEMM, DeepEP
  expert-parallel comms, speculative decoding, built on vLLM, targeting NVIDIA
  SM100 and Huawei Ascend 950. Three things are withheld: KV-cache management,
  prefill/decode disaggregation and load balancing — the layer where its cost
  advantage actually lives.
- **Mistral shipped Voxtral 3** (20 Oct): speech-to-text in 50+ languages,
  speech generation in 35+, and a voice-agent SDK on LiveKit promising sub-second
  turns; API now, on-premises by contact. Europe's first full voice stack.
- **Anthropic's draft IPO filing shows an $80.7 billion loss** — the first
  frontier lab heading for the public markets while burning at that scale;
  reported by press summarising the prospectus.
- **Google released TorchTPU** (19 Oct, open source): a native PyTorch backend
  for TPUs, with Hugging Face reporting 96.5% of tests passing — compute
  diversification beyond CUDA you can actually try.
- One clause: **Ornith 1.5 122B** hit Ollama with MIT weights and a vendor-reported
  SWE-bench Verified of 84.5 — small local models keep eating agent workloads.

## Geopolitics

- **Washington moved on DeepSeek directly**: DHS ordered DeepSeek products —
  including third-party services built on its models — out of US civilian agency
  systems (published 20 Oct), and the State Department sanctioned five Chinese
  entities tied to the Google trade-secrets case. First time an open-weights
  model family is formally treated as supply-chain risk, not just a competitor.
- **Chip tariffs took effect overnight**: 100% on most semiconductors for firms
  not committing to US production, with Apple exempt after pledging another
  $100bn domestically. Tariff policy is now an explicit industrial-placement
  lever.
- **The minerals gap survived the summit**: the truce extension expires **10
  Nov** with nothing resolved; China refines 19 of 20 key strategic minerals
  (IEA, ~70% average share, 94% of magnets) and its magnet exports to the US
  fell 13% year-on-year. From 1 Jan 2027 the Pentagon bans adversary-sourced
  metals with almost no waivers — a two-year fuse the US industry says it cannot
  fully meet.
- **Rubio in Mumbai**: an India–US trade deal is "about as close as you can be",
  with energy (Venezuelan crude via Reliance) as the sweetener — while Modi
  prepares his first China visit in seven years. India is being courted from
  both sides of the chip and minerals fence.

## Project ideas

- **Benchmark DeepInfer against a plain vLLM stack.** DeepInfer's speed and cost
  claims are hours old and unbenchmarked publicly. Rent one GPU node, serve
  V4.1-Flash-1017 both ways, publish tok/s, p50/p99 latency and $/M tokens with
  realistic cache-hit rates. First step: the DeepInfer README quickstart on a
  single SM100-class node, then one comparison table.
- **A voice ground station for a robot or drone.** Voxtral 3 plus PyTorch 2.15's
  MPS speedups (9.4 → 61 tok/s decoding on M3 Ultra) make fully-local,
  sub-second voice control plausible on a laptop. Bridge speech to a ROS 2 topic
  and fly/behave on voice. First step: one script that turns mic audio into a
  named ROS 2 action call.
- **An audit-evidence checker.** HackAudit's priority challenge is whether an
  AI-produced document qualifies as valid audit evidence under NEP 500. A small
  tool that scores an artifact against that standard would be both an entry and
  a product. First step: read the NEP 500 summary in the HackAudit rules and
  sketch the checklist as JSON.
