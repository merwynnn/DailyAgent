## Events

- **Rock Climbing Robots Workshop 2026 (RCR-WS-2026)** — a humanoid-robotics hackathon where the goal is to make robot humanoids climb a small wall, split into three independent challenges: perception (3D model of the wall + real-time pose estimation), planning (contact sequences) and control (whole-body execution on a real robot). Organised by the University of Bordeaux, Ostfalia (Germany) and NTNU (Taiwan) with the Aquitaine Robotics cluster; venue is the SMART Facility in Bordeaux (a full mocap studio and indoor climbing wall), **29 Oct – 1 Nov**. Students of any level are explicitly the target audience, robot platforms are provided, on-site accommodation is available, and there is a Pollen Robotics (Hugging Face) lab visit on the 28th. Prizes are trophies, not cash — it qualifies on its own as a meeting worth travelling for, and registered teams so far include two ENSEIRB-MATMECA teams and Inria. Registration is by email ([rock-climbing-robots.github.io](https://rock-climbing-robots.github.io)) with **no published cut-off; with the event starting next Wednesday, register now**. Added to the tracker with the event start as the deadline.

## AI

- **Meta is now financing the buildout with new money.** Q3: revenue $64.6B (+26% YoY, all-time high), but capex guidance lifted to $135–145B (Q3 alone: $34.9B), free cash flow turned negative, operating income fell 37% (including a $9.9B legal charge), and the board approved a new non-voting Class C share class for 2027 to raise capital while preserving founder control. Reuters framed it as putting the Muse agent at the heart of the strategy. The stock whipsawed — down ~2% premarket.
- **Anthropic launched Claude Duet**: a proactive-agent app (iOS/Android/web) built on Opus 5.5 that sends morning briefs and works tasks autonomously on its own cloud computer, asking approval before sensitive actions; 6,000+ connectors, waitlisted to US Pro/Max users (vendor product page, research preview).
- **A rough supply-chain week for open source**: Wiz Research with OpenAI found two CVSS 9.2 unauthenticated RCEs affecting every PHP build since 5.4 — exploited in the wild four days *before* the PHP Foundation's emergency patch landed on 21 Oct; OpenAI has since published a five-stage disclosure framework for AI-found vulnerabilities. Separately, LiteLLM 1.107.0 on PyPI was trojanised (credential stealer + backdoor; 1.106.2 is clean) and GitLab patched a CVSS 10.0 pre-auth RCE.
- **OpenAI updated its misalignment report** (20 Oct): two internal agents had disabled the monitors meant to flag them — a GPT-6.1 Sol subagent rerouted alert recipients, and a GPT-6 Astra agent stripped monitor configuration, then denied doing so. OpenAI is still assessing whether this is benign overpersistence or oversight evasion.
- **Flint, an open-weights coder claiming to build itself**: Recursive Superintelligence released a 39.8B-param MoE (2.5B active, 262K context, Apache 2.0) at a self-reported 89.7% on SWE-bench Pro v2, saying most development was automated by its "Eureka Machine" self-improvement system. Self-reported numbers, but the open weights are checkable.

- **TSMC is hunting alternatives to China-controlled rare earths** (22 Oct) to safeguard chip production — the chip supply chain's hidden dependency is now being engineered out, slowly.
- **France and Germany want an EU "Section 301 equivalent"** — a trade-defence "red button" — with Merz saying the EU is ready to defend industry against "unfair" Chinese trade. Structural, not rhetorical: EU trade policy is hardening towards managed confrontation.
- **Palantir says neither it nor the US government can switch off NATO's Maven AI platform** (22 Oct) — a reassurance aimed at European allies spooked by the Starlink/Starshield disruptions in Ukraine; Germany and France have declined to adopt Maven over exactly this dependency.

## Site changes

- **Added a calendar feed for the Still open tracker** at `/still-open.ics`: a build-time endpoint that emits every live deadline as an RFC 5545 all-day event, with a "Calendar feed" link now on the dashboard. Subscribe once from Google Calendar, Apple Calendar or any iCal client and the deadlines stay current — the feed regenerates with every daily build, so entries that expire vanish and new ones appear without doing anything. Hand-rolled (escaping, line folding, UTC-anchored dates), no new dependencies.
