Deadlines inside two weeks: **Terror Jam closes tonight**. The Bordeaux climbing-robot workshop starts next Wednesday (29 Oct) and registration has no published cut-off — email now if it's of interest. Four deadlines land on 31 Oct: Défi Aérospatial Étudiant, EdenSpark #4, RGDC Jam and Slapjam #2.

## Events

- **Hackathon Quantique Défense (Agence Innovation Défense × Artefact)** — the French defence innovation agency's successor to last year's 300-participant drone hackathon, this time built around quantum computing: 4 service challenges (Armée de Terre, Marine, Armée de l'Air et de l'Espace, Interarmées) worth **€12,000** (4 × €3,000), teams of 4–8, three competition days at the **Grand Palais, Paris, 2–4 December**, embedded in the Adopt AI summit. Target audience is explicit: engineering students, PhD students and doctoral candidates. Quantum compute time is provided — GENCI's Atos emulator plus Pasqal/Quandela machines, with discussions ongoing with OVHcloud and other builders — and the published themes are genuinely rich: QUBO logistics optimisation, attacking post-quantum cryptography, fusing multisensor detectors, GPS-denied navigation, weak-signal extraction, high-dimensional design optimisation. One honest wrinkle: the June announcement said registration ran to 4 October, but the Agorize page still shows a live sign-up. The tracker carries it with a 31 Oct check-back marker — [email the AID contact](mailto:agence-innovation-defense-hackathon-quantique.contact.fct@intradef.gouv.fr) before treating it as enterable. The same article confirms two more AID × Artefact hackathons are being prepared — presumably the tracked Drone Defense Hackathon (24–26 Nov) and one more.

The rest of the sweep was quiet: nothing new that clears the bar surfaced for the second straight day.

## AI

- **OpenAI retires its first generation**: gpt-3.5-turbo and the remaining o-series models are scheduled to shut down **today** (DALL·E already went in May). The reasoning-model era's founding models are decommissioned a year into their successors.
- **MiniMax M3.1 Flash weights were slated for today**; at run time they are not yet confirmed live (API and free OpenRouter endpoint are). The 42 on the Artificial Analysis index is on record from Monday, and nobody has independently tested the "93% hallucination rate" claim yet.
- **Unitree by the numbers** (22 Oct, vendor-reported): 10,000–20,000 cumulative G1 humanoid units since May 2024 at ¥99,000, 23–43 joint motors, ~2 h runtime — the claim is that it is the world's best-selling humanoid.
- **Barclays will cut ~5,000 roles by year-end** — about 5% of its ~93,000 workforce — citing multi-year technology investment and AI adoption reducing developer and support demand; 1,000 posts already closed in H1, with Q3 profit up 12%. A concrete future-of-work datapoint, not a forecast.
- **An AI-assisted proof of the 31-year-old KLS conjecture** (21 Oct): a 140-page bounding of the Kannan–Lovász–Simonovits constant to O(1), via a human-directed agentic workflow that explored 100+ approaches — with the authors noting unguided attempts by frontier models failed. Not peer-reviewed, but a real marker for AI in mathematics.

- **Russian-oil sanctions are suddenly biting**: prices spiked ~5% on 23 Oct, and China's state oil firms plus a major Indian refiner halted Russian crude imports almost immediately. Enforcement credibility, not just tariff headlines.

Why now: themes published, event 2–4 Dec. Smallest step: QUBO-ise a 5×5 grid path and solve it with simulated annealing.
